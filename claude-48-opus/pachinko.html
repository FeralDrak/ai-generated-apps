📖 How to Play

Pachinko, but make it a roguelike.
  • Aim & drop: move your mouse over the board and click (or press Space) to drop the loaded orb. The dotted line previews the bounce.
  • Pegs: cyan pegs add the orb's damage to its pot. Gold crit pegs raise the orb's multiplier (×2, ×3…). Hit crit pegs early!
  • Slots: where the orb falls multiplies the whole pot — aim for the ×3 center.
  • Combat: the pot is dealt to your targeted enemy (click to switch). Kill everything before their ⚔️ timer hits 0.
  • Pegs refresh when the board thins out. Some enemies have 🛡️ block that reduces each hit.
  • Between fights: climb the map, collect orbs & relics, shop, rest. Beat the 🗿 Golem to win.
